# Petalcore ID

A photo-identification tool for plants. Find out what a plant might be from a photo using Pl@ntNet.

## How it works

After a photo is submitted, the backend sends it to Pl@ntNet and receives possible plant matches. Petalcore shows those suggestions with their match scores. The results are suggestions, so they may not always be correct. Petalcore does not keep uploaded photos.

## How to use it

Open Petalcore Platform and choose **ID**. Choose a JPG or PNG photo, or take one if your device supports it. Pick the plant part in the photo, or leave it on automatic, then select **Identify this plant** to view the possible matches.
